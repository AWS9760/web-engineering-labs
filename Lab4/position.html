Five positions, three ideas

Coloured squares. Then scroll to the pink panel.

1. Static — simple flow

HTML order. Nothing is offset.

2. Relative vs Absolute

Relative — middle box moves. Dotted ghost = the seat it still owns. Neighbours stay put.

Absolute — mint box leaves the row. Neighbours close the gap. It is measured from the red dot (parent's top-left). Parent has position: relative.

3. Fixed vs Sticky — scroll now

The mint square at the bottom-right is fixed. It stays on the window the whole time.

The mint square in the pink panel is sticky. It sticks to the top while pink is on screen, then scrolls away with pink.

Keep scrolling inside this pink parent…

Sticky is still here, stuck to the top.
